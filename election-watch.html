Pre-Election & Post-Election Watch

Compare documented campaign commitments, transition messaging, and official actions after inauguration. An archived page proves what appeared on that page, not why it changed.

Evidence first: an item is not a broken promise merely because it has not yet been acted upon. Dates and sources matter.

Methodology & evidence

Each entry identifies its period, source, date and evidence status. Primary sources are preferred. Campaign promises are not laws; transition announcements are not executive orders. Official actions must be verified against state records. Updates are curated, not live monitoring.
