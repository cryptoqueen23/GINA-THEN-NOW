07

How this project works

METHODOLOGY

This project compares public campaign materials with official legislative records. The goal is a traceable record readers can audit for themselves.

Archived campaign statements

Historical campaign claims are tied to dated archive captures when available. A Wayback calendar alone establishes capture availability, not the content of a page.

Current positions

Current campaign statements come from the saved campaign homepage and are labeled with the date checked.

Legislative votes

House floor and committee votes are labeled separately. Individual votes are not inferred from party, sponsorship, attendance, committee membership, or voice votes.

Website changes

If content disappears from a particular page, we say it was no longer displayed there. A change in presentation does not by itself establish motive or a policy reversal.

Secondary research

Ballotpedia and advocacy scorecards can identify statements and votes worth investigating, but official Texas records control the verified voting database.

CORRECTIONS
If an error is identified, it will be reviewed against the underlying primary source and corrected.
